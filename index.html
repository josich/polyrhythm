How this works

The rings

  • Ring 1 is the click, at the tempo you set.
  • More rings make a polyrhythm. Four at most.

The dial

  • Tap a dot to cycle it: on → silent → accent → on.
  • An accent plays louder and brighter, and is drawn with a ring.
  • Hold and drag a ring to offset it.
  • The middle starts and stops.

Tempo

  • Tap the number to type a tempo.
  • The slider gives half its length to 1–120.
  • The note row says what one click is worth: = 200 is 200 eighths a minute.
  • Switching notes keeps the speed and converts the number.
  • Tap tempo sets it by feel.
  • Count-in plays three beats first.

Per ring

  • Divisions per rotation.
  • ×2 ×3 ×4 subdivide each beat.
  • A subdivision sounds on its own, beat on or off.
  • Offset nudges the ring off the beat.
  • Each ring has its own sound, level and mute.
  • Tap the tab already open to fold the card away.

Practice session

  • Raises the tempo by a step every few loops.
  • Random silent loops.
  • Swaps patterns each step.
  • Plan shows what is scheduled.
  • Repeat plays the plan again; out and back runs it there and home.
  • The middle pauses; Stop ends it.

Sequence

  • The session plays the sequence in order.
  • + adds a pattern. Tap a box to change it.
  • Drag a box onto another to swap.
  • Score writes it out as notation, one bar each. Empty sequence: the wheel.

Tapping

  • Game: listen to one loop, then tap the wheel on every dot before it ends.
  • A stray tap or a dot left over costs the loop; clear it to climb. 100 levels, 3 rhythms each.
  • Tap pattern builds a ring: tap the wheel.
  • On ring 1 the count sets divisions and the speed sets tempo.
  • On others each tap drops a dot where the hand is.
  • Placed dots never move. Tap one again to remove it.

Keeping it

  • Everything autosaves.
  • Advanced shows or hides the extras.
  • Presets: classic ratios, rhythms by region, and your own.
  • The triangle folds a section; a folded one blinks while it runs.
