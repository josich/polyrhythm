How this works

The wheel

  • Ring 1 is the click, at the tempo you set.
  • More rings make a polyrhythm. Four at most.
  • Score writes it out as notation, one bar each.
  • Tap a dot to cycle it: on → silent → accent → on.
  • To offset a ring, hold it until it lights, then drag.

Tempo

  • Set the BPM between clicks of Ring 1.
  • The note row says what one click is worth: = 200 is 200 eighths a minute.
  • Switching notes keeps the speed and converts the number.
  • Tap tempo sets it by feel.
  • Count-in plays three beats first.

Ring tabs

  • Divisions per rotation.
  • ×2 ×3 ×4 subdivide each beat.
  • Offset nudges the ring off the beat.
  • Each ring has its own sound, level and mute.
  • Tap pattern creates the beats as you play them.

Practice session

  • Program a practice session.
  • Random silent loops.
  • Swaps patterns each step.
  • Plan shows what is scheduled.
  • Repeat plays the plan again.
  • Out and back runs it there and home.

Play time

  • Game: it counts you in and plays one grey loop. Listen.
  • Then tap the wheel on every dot before the loop ends.
  • Clear the loop to climb a level.
  • A stray tap or a dot left over costs the loop; the dots come back at the bar line.
  • Pause stops the clock.
  • Calibrate measures the device's own delay: ten clicks, tap along. Run it again when the note asks.

Sequence

  • The practice session can play the sequence in order.
  • + adds a pattern. Tap a box to change it.
  • Drag a box onto another to swap.

Patterns

  • Everything autosaves.
  • Presets: save your patterns.
  • Rhythms: world patterns.
  • Classic ratios: basic patterns to start from.
