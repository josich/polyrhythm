The plan

How this works

The rings

  • Ring 1 is the click.
  • It ticks at the BPM you set.
  • A polyrhythm can be created adding more rings. Four rings maximum.

The dial

  • Click a dot to silence that beat.
  • Hold and Drag a ring to offset it.

Tempo

  • Click the number to type a tempo.
  • Tap tempo sets it by feel.
  • Count-in plays three beats before the rings.

Per ring

  • Sets the number of divisions.
  • ×2 ×3 ×4 subdivide to create accents.
  • A subdivision sounds on its own, beat off or not.
  • Offset nudges it off the beat.
  • Each ring has its own sound, level and mute.
  • Tap the tab already showing to fold these away.

Practice session

  • Change tempo by small increments each amount of loops.
  • Random silent loops.
  • Patterns played randomly each increment of time.
  • The repeat button plays the whole plan over again.
  • Out and back runs it to the end and home again.

Sequence

  • The practice session plays the sequence in order.
  • Press + to put a pattern in the row.
  • Drag one box onto another to swap them.
  • Tap a box to change or remove that step.

Tapping

  • Practise a rhythm with no instrument at all.
  • Tap along grades every tap on the bar under the dial.
  • Teal is tight, gold is close, pink is off.
  • Your taps stay on the dial, so you can see if you run early.
  • Tap pattern on a ring builds it instead: tap the wheel.
  • On ring 1 the count sets the divisions and the speed the tempo.
  • On the others each tap drops a dot where the hand is.
  • The grid works itself out, and placed dots never move.
  • After the first loop, tapping a dot again takes it off.
  • Press it again to keep them.

Keeping it

  • Between sessions autosave.
  • Advanced buttons shows/hide some features.
  • Preset chips are classic ratios; name your own below.
  • The triangle folds a section down to its name.
  • A folded section blinks while something inside it runs.
  • Each region of rhythms opens and closes the same way.
